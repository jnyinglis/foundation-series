Encyclopedia Galactica

Radole

RADOLE. Independent Trader world of the Periphery, tidally locked to its primary. The sunward hemisphere exceeds the boiling point of water; the far hemisphere is ice to a depth that has never been measured. Habitation is confined to the twilight strip, a band some forty kilometres wide, where the Traders' settlement, TWILIGHT CITY, is built into and hung from the cliffs of the terminator. The port is a natural cavern. The population at the time of writing is uncertain, as the Traders do not report it.

Radole gained importance after the loss of the Four Kingdoms' routes, as the last Foundation port before the old Imperial frontier. Vessels bound for the Normannic Sector or the Arcturus worlds clear from Radole or do not clear at all. The city's cantina is reported in several Trader memoirs as the only warm room on the planet. The Encyclopedia has not been able to verify this.

Plate: section through Radole at the terminator, with the twilight strip and the cliff city. Encyclopedia Galactica, 116th edition, Terminus. Reproduced by permission of the Board.

Riose, Bel · The Riose Crisis

RIOSE, BEL. Last of the Imperial generals. In the years before F.E. 200 Riose led the Empire's final expedition against the Foundation, winning every engagement he fought, including the action off HORLEGGOR. He was recalled by the Emperor Cleon II and executed. Psychohistorians consider the recall, not the campaign, to be the crisis: a strong general could not be left to win, and a weak Emperor could not let him. SELDON's recording in the Time Vault had predicted the outcome. Riose is remembered on the frontier worlds as a man the Empire killed for competence.

Arcturus Sector

Former sector capital. Forty worlds answered to the viceroy at Arcturus; at the time of writing the viceroy answers to whichever fleet is within three jumps. The Spaceship-and-Sun is still flown. Tithes are assessed on arriving vessels and are commonly paid in Foundation manufactures.
